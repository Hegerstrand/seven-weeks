# How to Play

*Printable leaflet - two pages. Print this file directly (browser print or any Markdown-to-PDF
tool), or read it on screen. Replaces the old `leaflet-how-to-play-A4-portrait.svg` /
`leaflet-how-to-play-2-A4-portrait.svg` pair - same wording, plain text instead of hand-laid SVG.*

Seven weeks. Thirty-five days. One project standing between your team and the deadline - your
team commits five days at a time, resolves them all at once, then closes the week and moves on.
From week 2, Events crash into the week you already committed to - spend Adaptability to
re-plan around them or cancel them outright. Deliver every Feature before Week 7 closes to win
the project - but hit a Team Score of 80+ and you didn't just finish it, you built a team that
could survive doing it again: Sustainable High Performance.

## Page 1 of 2 · Setup and Week 1

### Setting up

*About fifteen minutes, and the first part is the best part.*

1. Deal each player five habitus parts at random and **assemble the figure** onto the Specimen
   board.
2. Take the matching **NEED** tile (your VALUES) and **HERO** tile (your PRESSURE RESPONSE), and slot those
   too.
3. Turn over board **THE ZONE**. Place your figure's tile on the grid: your Legs' row × your
   Item's column.
4. Read the row/column rule and what **Learn**/**Teach** do in one line each - no detail needed
   yet.
5. Pick one **PROJECT** card. Turn **THE PROJECT** face up and seat it - it names features A-F.
6. Read the project and its six features together - complexity, LOCK tiles across **D** and
   **F**, what unlocks what.
7. Go through the **Activity cards** that come out now: **Work, Coordinate, Celebrate**. The
   rest wait for later weeks.

**Finish the room**

- Only **THE PROJECT** and **THE ZONE** start face up. **SHIT HAPPENS** and **THE TEAM** stay
  text side up; **THE PAYOFF** and **STANDING** stay boxed.
- **Motivation 3** for every character. Week marker on Week 1. Shuffle the Events but don't draw
  yet.
- *Everything on the covered boards already sits at 2. It just isn't your problem for a few more
  weeks.*

### Week 1

*Deliberately almost trivial - lay five cards, resolve them, close the week.*

*Only **THE PROJECT** and **THE ZONE** are face up. Shit Happens, Adapt and the Ledger wait for
week 2; the Specimen panel on your person board isn't read until week 3.*

**1 · Plan the week** - Lay **five Activity cards** across Monday to Friday. Put a character disc
on each - that person's Skill, Knowledge and Capital apply to that day. One person may take
several days, or none. A slot may instead take a **Flex marker**, giving back a day someone
worked as Overtime. *Then stop. Nothing has resolved.*

**2 · The week runs** - Resolve all **five** cards. **Order does not matter** - the week happened
at once, not one day at a time.

**3 · Close the week** - **Bank** any feature that finished (lift its lock, if any, and pay
Capital). Everyone then **loses 1 Motivation** - the weekly grind. Nobody empties out on week 1
alone, so there is nothing else to check yet. *Move the week marker one step closer to the
deadline. That's the whole week - three steps, no exceptions.*

> bank before the grind - it's the only spend this week.

**From week 2** - **THE TEAM** turns face up: **the Ledger** - Trust, Capability, Shared
Practice, Adaptability - starts being read at Close the Week. Nothing else about a week changes
yet.

*A week is five days, and you choose them. That's the entire idea, before anything else gets
added to it.*

**Flip this sheet when Week 1 closes → Week 2 on is on the back**

## Page 2 of 2 · Weeks after that, and how it ends

*The same five days, but the table has more to say about them with every week that passes.*

### The pulse, from week 2 on

*The same five steps, every week until week 7 closes.*

**1 · Plan the week** - Lay **five Activity cards** across Monday to Friday, one character disc
each. *Then stop. Nothing has resolved.*

**2 · Shit happens** - Draw **two Event cards, face up** - and **do not resolve them yet**. They
are threats, aimed at the week you already committed. *A day spent on the wrong thing is simply
lost.*

**3 · Adapt** - Spend **Adaptability**: each 1 replaces one card, or pays an Event's ADAPT number
to cancel it outright.

**4 · The week runs** - Resolve whatever survived Adapt, then all five cards. **Order does not
matter** - the week happened at once.

**5 · Close the week** - **Bank** completed features (lift any lock, pay Capital) → **walk the
Ledger** coins, at most one step each, and flip any that reached its ring → **Motivation as one
net number**: −1 for the grind - every week, ±1 per matching Event category per Torso (check
who was hit and who was immune), +1 if the Need was met.

> bank first - it pays the capital the ledger needs.
> apply motivation as one number, then check for zero.

**Week 2:** the Ledger + Shit Happens/Adapt, together.
**Week 3:** the Development + Specimen panel - Capital, Need, Overtime, Flex.
**Week 4:** Standing.
**Week 5:** Combos - two named triggers in the same week now pay a small bonus on top of what
they already did alone (rulebook §18.1).

### How it ends

*The project ends when Week 7 closes.*

**Team Score** = (features × 10) + (4 tracks, max 20) + (Motivation) − (10 × quits) − (2 ×
unrepaid Flex)

| Outcome | Condition | |
|---|---|---|
| **Failure** | Not everything delivered. | *The score doesn't matter.* |
| **Heroic Success** | All six banked, score under 80. | *The project made it. The team didn't.* |
| **High Performance** | All six banked, score 80+. | *You built a team as well.* |
