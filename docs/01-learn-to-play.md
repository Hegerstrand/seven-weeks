# Learn to Play

**Level 1** of the game's [reading order](../README.md#reading-order) - the streamlined,
illustrated walkthrough. This is the standard name for this tier across the hobby (Wingspan,
Pandemic, and most Stonemaier/Days of Wonder games ship a "Learn to Play" booklet separate from
the full rules) - it teaches you enough to make your first real decision in five minutes,
nothing more.

## Setup, in order

1. **Deal habitus parts, one per row, five parts each - Base, Legs, Torso, Head, Item.**
   Nobody picks; you're handed a person. Ninety seconds, no decisions.
2. **Put one Project card in its seat.** It names features A-F. Lock tiles go across D and F.
3. **Out come three cards: Work, Coordinate, Celebrate.** That's all of Week 1's toolkit.
   Everything else arrives later, at the week it's actually needed.

## The pulse of a week

**1 - Plan the week.** Lay five Activity cards across Monday-Friday. Put a character disc on
each - that person's Skill, Knowledge and Habitus apply to that day. One person may take
several days, or none.

**2 - Shit happens.** Draw two Event cards, face up. Don't resolve them yet - they're threats,
not facts.

**3 - Adapt.** If you have Adaptability to spend, you may cancel a threat or swap what someone's
doing. If not, you play the week exactly as you laid it.

**4 - Resolve, and close the week.** Everything happens - order never matters, the week is a
set, not a sequence.

Do that seven times. Week 7 is the deadline.

## The one thing worth knowing before you start

**Work is the only card that moves the project - and that's the trap.** Coordinate, Align,
Learn, Reflect, Celebrate don't build anything directly, but skip them for too long and the
team itself starts to fail under you, long before the deadline does.
