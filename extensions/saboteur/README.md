# Extension: Saboteur

**Requires:** the base game (rulebook v0.31 or later). **Changes:** what happens when a
character's Motivation reaches 0. In the base game they **quit** (§13) and the seat sits empty
for a week before a rushed newcomer arrives. With this pack they **do not quit**: they turn
**Saboteur**, stay at the table, and change objective from *deliver the project* to *make it
fail*.

**Status: drafted 2026-10-08, never played.** Every number below is a first guess.

## Why this pack exists
The 2026-09-25 playtest left "consequence for quitting (lose condition, cascade, score hit)"
open (see `game/playtests/README.md`). Quitting removes a player from the game at the moment
they are already having the worst week. This pack keeps them playing, turns the cost of burnout
into something the whole table feels, and makes "look after each other" a reason rather than a
rule. It also gives the weekly grind (§5) a second face: a team that never celebrates does not
just lose points, it grows an opponent.

## What this pack changes
- **§13 Quit** is replaced by **Turning** (see [rules/addendum.md](rules/addendum.md)).
- **Replacement and Onboarding (§12, §13)** do not trigger: nobody leaves, so no newcomer
  arrives. (Fired characters still leave and are still replaced.)
- **Team Score (§16):** a character who turned counts as having quit (-10) for as long as they
  stay turned; they add 0 Motivation. A character who is won back (see the addendum) does not.
- **Victory (§16):** each Saboteur wins if the project fails. If the team delivers, the
  Saboteurs lose, whatever the Team Score.

## Components needed (not yet designed)
- A **SABOTEUR** marker: lay the figure on its side, or a flat tile on the person board.
- A small **Saboteur actions** card (the three actions in the addendum), one per Saboteur.

## Open questions
- Is the turn **open** (everyone sees it, as written) or **hidden** (the Saboteur keeps playing
  normally and the table finds out by what goes wrong)? Open is far simpler; hidden is closer to
  the idea of a quiet burnout.
- Is the team-delivers-so-the-Saboteur-loses rule too harsh for the player who burned out first?
  A softer version scores the Saboteur on how much damage they did instead of pass/fail.
- Does the Saboteur still read their **Need** and take part in Teach/Learn? As written they do
  not.
- Balance: three Saboteur actions a week against a team that is already short a pair of hands.
  Likely too strong at 4 players, which is the reason the win-back route exists.
