# Saboteur - rules addendum (draft, never played)

Replaces the **Quit** bullet in rulebook §13 and the "any character at 0 quits" check in §5 and
§6.1 (step 7 of the week, Motivation). Everything else in the base game is unchanged.

## Turning
At step 7 of the week the net Motivation number is applied as normal. **A character who
ends on 0 does not quit. They turn Saboteur.**

- Lay their figure on its side (or place the SABOTEUR marker on their board). Their Motivation
  stays at 0 and **cannot rise** while they are a Saboteur: Celebrate and Need do nothing for
  them, and they take no Motivation losses.
- They **stay at the table**. Their seat does not empty, there is **no replacement** and **no
  Onboarding** (§12, §13).
- They stop taking part in the team: they play no Hero, take no Capital, do not teach or learn,
  and their Need is not read. Activity cards are the team's anyway (§6.0), so nothing is taken
  from the week's five days.
- Their Capital stays where it was and still counts for Standing (§5.1).

## Each week the Saboteur does one thing
Chosen **after Plan the week** (§6.1, step 1) and before Shit Happens.

1. **Obstruct.** Turn one of the week's five laid cards face down. It produces nothing and
   counts as a lost day. *The team may cancel it by spending 1 adapt token.*
2. **Spread doubt.** Move Trust down 1 (minimum 0).
3. **Pile on.** Draw **one extra Event** this week (three instead of two). The team answers it
   in Adapt like any other Event.

A Saboteur may skip a week. Two Saboteurs may not pick the same action in the same week.

## Winning them back
A Saboteur becomes a normal character again, with **2 Motivation**, at step 7 of the week if
**either**:
- **The team played both an Engagement and a Celebrate** that week and Trust is **3 or more**; or
- **another character spent a Hero on them** (Support or Own It only; the Hero is spent as
  normal and needs 3 Motivation).

Once won back they are no longer counted as having quit. They may turn again later.

## Scoring and victory
- **Team Score (§16):** each character still turned at the end counts as one who quit (-10) and
  adds 0 Motivation. Fired characters are scored as in the base game.
- **Project Failure** (the Tower is short at the end of week 7): **every Saboteur wins**, and
  the rest of the team loses as usual.
- **The team delivers** (Heroic Success or Sustainable High Performance): the team wins and
  **every Saboteur loses**.
