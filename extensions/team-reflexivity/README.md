# Extension: Team Reflexivity (history of the old pool-flip version)

**Status (2026-10-10): the old version below was cut, and Team Reflexivity is back in the base
game in a new form: earned by Reflect, not by the pool.** In the base game now: when a Reflect
resolves, the team may bank its +1 Adaptability token in one of three HABIT wells beside the pool
on SHIT HAPPENS, labelled objectives, strategy and process (West's three objects of reflection),
if the week gave it evidence for that well, and it names one change for next week. Three filled
wells turn the coin over for good, and Customer Events then cost 1 less. See rulebook §18. The rest
of this file is the record of the old rules and the alternatives.

> ## The pool-flip Team Reflexivity ("the pool holds 4") is no longer in the game.

## Why it was removed
Adaptability stopped being a Ledger track and became a **currency**: a pool of adapt tokens
(cap 6, start 3) on SHIT HAPPENS, earned by Align, Workshop and Reflect, spent on Events. A Habit
is a coin that walks a track and is turned over for good at the ring. A pool that is spent down
and refilled has no track to walk and no ring to reach, so a permanent flip tied to "the pool
holds 4" no longer fitted: the thing that earns the Habit is the same thing you are meant to
spend.

## What it was (the rules as they stood in rulebook v0.33)
- **Habit:** Team Reflexivity, from West 1996 (teams that overtly reflect on their objectives and
  processes, and change them, outperform teams that don't).
- **Acquired:** the first time the Adaptability pool holds **4 or more tokens at close of week**
  (§6.1 step 6, "then flip Team Reflexivity if the pool now holds 4 or more"). Permanent: it never
  turns back over, even if the pool is spent afterwards.
- **Effect:** every **Customer**-category Event costs **1 less** Adaptability to cancel
  (minimum 1). It was the Habit that discounted the Customer category, in the same table as
  Psychological Safety (People), Who Knows What (Environment) and Shared Repertoire (Management).
- **Quality Torso:** Team Reflexivity was the Habit that **vindicated** a Quality character. While
  the team had it, the Quality Need counted as met every week (so the -3 for a week with no Work
  never applied), and the character gained +1 Capital when it arrived.
- **Starting pool:** the start of 3 tokens was set partly so the pool could not begin at 4,
  which would have flipped the Habit for nothing.
- **Board:** on THE TEAM it had its own row (Adaptability, `#B5C7CF`) with **one** coin well and a
  ring, captioned "TURN IT OVER THE FIRST TIME THE POOL HOLDS 4 AT WEEK END", with the name
  "Team Reflexivity" and "WEST 1996" on the right. For a short time it lived as a HABIT lane
  (36 x 49 mm, `#B5C7CF`, one coin well r8 with a ring r10.5) beside the pool and the HERO lane on
  SHIT HAPPENS.

## What still carries its trace (not yet reprinted)
- **Seven Customer Event cards** print "HABIT: TEAM REFLEXIVITY" and a cost line: UNCLEAR
  REQUIREMENT (still costs 1), NEGATIVE FEEDBACK (costs 1 instead of 2), SCOPE CREEP (costs 1
  instead of 2), PRIORITY CHANGES (still costs 1), THE DATE MOVES UP (costs 2 instead of 3), THE
  DASHBOARD (costs 2 instead of 3), ALWAYS REACTING (still costs 1). Files:
  `cards-event-01-09`, `cards-event-10-18`, `cards-event-19-21`.
- **The Quality Need tile** on `cards-need-hero-44x36mm-A4-landscape.svg` still reads "Work added
  this week, or Team Reflexivity: +1 Motivation".
- Older notes, specs and playtest logs still mention it. That is history, not a rule.

## How to bring it back
Pick one; they are not equally cheap.

1. **As it was.** A permanent coin, flipped once. Needs a place for the coin (a HABIT lane beside
   the pool on SHIT HAPPENS is the smallest change), the §18 table row, the §6.1 close step, the
   Customer row of the §6.1 category table, and the Quality Need and Torso table restored. The
   seven Customer cards and the Quality tile then already match.
2. **As a live state, with no coin.** While the pool holds 4 or more tokens when the Events are
   drawn, Customer Events cost 1 less. It follows the pool instead of being earned once, so it
   fits a currency better and needs no component. The seven cards would need a different HABIT
   wording.
3. **Tied to Reflect instead.** Reflect is the Activity that most literally is team reflexivity.
   For example: a team that has played Reflect in three different weeks has it. This ties the
   Habit to an Activity, not to a spendable pool, but it adds bookkeeping.

## Open questions
- Is there still a reason for the Customer category to have a Habit when the other three do?
  Without one, Customer Events are the only category a team can never make cheaper, which makes
  them the ones worth cancelling with tokens.
- Without it, Quality is the only Torso with no vindicating Habit besides Recognition. Does the
  Quality character need another route to a met Need?
