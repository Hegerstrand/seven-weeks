## Notes - play test

### Players 
5-6, hobbyist/casual

### What we tested: 
Full play through

### Summary of the play through

Setup
- Introduced the game's concept
- Built characters
- Turned over The Zone before players chose a tool
- Explained the point system - only features at this point with a hint to more
- Distributed motivations
- Introduced the three types of activity cards: Learn, Celebrate, Work
  - Learn was confusing for the players, but then they saw they could get the info by reading - but there was a lot to read

Week 1
- Learn, Celebrate, Work x3
- It wasn't clear to the players that they should lay a whole week of activities before resolving them, they wanted to go straight to resolving Learn
- Celebrate text didn't make sense for week 1 because they didn't know what the grind was yet
- It was quickly agreed that they should start with Feature A
- It was a lot for players to remember to check the bonuses or penalties from a player's habitus
- Various texts on the habitus cards don't make sense yet
- The week was resolved
- Points for habits were explained and the extra card on The Zone was flipped
- There was agreement among players that they should focus on getting the team out of the penalty zones for e.g. Shared Practice

Week 2
- Learn, Teach, Celebrate, Coordinate x2
  - i.e. absolutely no work, because all focus was on getting out of the penalty zone in habits
- The order to resolve Shit Happens and the week wasn't clear, a checklist would help
- There was confusion on what "at the end of the week means" in the context of players quitting
- Wording should be consistent and clear, e.g. "leave" OR "quit", also it would be helpful to bold important words like those
- A lot of time was spend this week on stacking
- Should it be possible to coordinate twice in one week?
- There was again a lot to keep track of, including the habitus bonuses and penalties, so quite a lot was missed by players

Week 3
- Learn, Celebrate, Teach, Align, Workshop
- Again focus on stats - motivation and habits
- Players intuitively flipped a work card that didn't count that week
- It wasn't clear that the grind should apply before events

Week 4
- Plan, Work x4
- Adaptability spending was not clear, it was first here that it was really used to cancel the effects of events

Week 5
- Plan, Work x3, Celebrate
- It was discussed whether an event that isn't dealt with (with adaptability or hero) keep showing up in subsequent weeks until it's dealt with? So events could end up stacking if they're not handled
- Event handling was starting to function well

Week 6
- Demo, Teach, Learn, Align, Celebrate

Week 7
- Plan, Work x4  

Conclusion
- Full points, everyone was happy
- Nobody used overtime

### Player feedback
- The text on some cards was very small
- There was a lot of text
- Week 1 was already difficult
- A checklist for every week's would be very useful so that things are done in the right order and players remember to check for bonuses and penalties etc.
- One player wondered if the habit track could show a bonus from 2 and above rather than a penalty from 1 and less. It became a priority to not be penalised, but is it better to be rewarded for doing extra? Priorities could also be guided with deadlines on features so that players don't start focusing only on habits
- Some wording was unclear, e.g. leave/quit - do they mean the same thing?
- There was a lot of focus on understanding all of the mechanics
  - A lot of time was spent on that in the first rounds
  - Maybe too much information is available too soon
  - Some text or complexity could be removed
- This game would be too complex for people who haven't played board games before
- The Zone was uoverskuelig
- It could be adjusted by considering a good leader's perpective:
  - It costs something to invest in the team
  - There are deadlines on features that need to be met
  - Investing in the team could also trigger good things, e.g. events

### Observations
- Remember to introduce the game's concept at the very beginning: to deliver a project... plus more
- It was a smoother process for the facilitator to lay out each character trait at a time for players to choose from
- The points for building the team only become relevant some time after the introduction
  - Only the points for features can be understood at the beginning of week 1
  - Explain the new type of points when The Team gets flipped?
  - Have it explicitly written clearly on The Team when it gets flipped?
- The shape of the track symbols should be different so it's clear they work differently - you can have 1-5 motivation tokens but only one capital token that moves up and down
- In general carefully consider the text that is on the cards, especially in week 1, because some things won't make sense yet
  - E.g. delete the reference to "the grind" on the Celebrate activity, or make sure to explain concepts like the grind in time
  - Lots of text in the habitus doesn't make sense in the first weeks and leads to player confusion/information overload
  - If teach does nothing then don't include teach in week 1
- There's a general demand for a checklist for every week so that players know exactly which order to resolve things in and which things they need to remember to keep track of, e.g. bonuses or penalties from habitus
- Reflect was revealed at week 2 but quickly removed and decided to be included from week 3
- Players spent a lot of time on weeks 1 and 2, probably because there was so much information and so many mechanics to understand, but week three was quicker and the rest were extremely fast
- Should there be consequences if someone quits?
  - Lose condition
  - Minus points at final scoring
  - Everyone else on the team is affected in e.g. motivation (cascading quitting due to motivation dropping?)
  - DARK MODE where the player that quit can sabotage the project (maybe a bit much, possible expansion lol)
- Should it cost something to do something other than work? Like in the real world, where learning and celebrating does have a cost, which is important to a leader and does need to be balanced
- Should there be deadlines on features? In real life it wouldn't be acceptable to have what happened in weeks 2 and 3, there would be a push to deliver features and balance that with building the team
- One player's hero could be used more than once, which was OP
- It was also OP that the requirement for one hero was to "have 3" motivation (rather than it cost 3 motivation)
- There was a lot of enjoyment of the references and joking about the situations that arose, so it was clear that it was very relatable in many ways
- Should adaptability be adjusted?
  - A currency
  - A set amount where when it's gone it's gone
  - Should it also cost something to use adaptability, e.g. time
  - Adaptability could also be an action that needs to be planned into the week in advance
- The players really optimised their strategy once they understood that it was important to get habits, but it meant weeks were either fully spent on working or fully spent on anything but working, and most features were delivered at the end
