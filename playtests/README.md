## 2026-09-25 - session 2, first full 7-week playthrough
Raw notes: `playtests/2026-09-25.md` on the remote (`Hegerstrand/seven-weeks`) - pull it. This
entry is the design response.

- **Players:** 5-6, hobbyist/casual.
- **What we tested:** full playthrough, v0.30. The facilitator's staging drifted from rulebook
  §3.0: week 1 offered Learn, Celebrate and Work (rulebook: Work, Coordinate, Celebrate), Teach
  was in play early, and Reflect was revealed in week 2 then pulled until week 3.
- **What worked:** relatable - lots of joking about the Events; Event handling "started to
  function well" by week 5; weeks 3-7 ran fast once the mechanics were understood; full points
  and a happy table at the end; nobody used Overtime.
- **What broke / was confusing:**
  - Too much to learn at once: weeks 1-2 were spent on mechanics; habitus text meant nothing
    in week 1; the Celebrate card mentioned "the grind" before anyone knew it; card text too
    small; too complex for non-board-gamers.
  - No order of play to follow, so habitus bonuses/penalties and the grind-vs-Events order
    were missed. Players asked for a per-week checklist.
  - **Exploit:** the habit penalty zones became the whole game. Weeks 1-3 were all Learn,
    Teach, Celebrate and Coordinate with zero Work; then Work-only weeks; most features landed
    at the end. Nothing punished a week with no Work.
  - One player's Hero was used more than once. That is the Competitive Base's reusable Hero,
    which the designer likes and wants kept; the "have 3 Motivation" requirement being a
    threshold, not a cost, was also noted as strong.
  - Adaptability was unclear: first really used in week 4; unclear what it is.
  - Tracking learned skills on the Zone "is close to impossible" at the table.
  - Wording: leave/quit, and "at the end of the week" for a quit.
  - Track symbols should differ (1-5 Motivation tokens vs one Capital marker).
- **Rule changes made as a result (rulebook v0.31):**
  1. Adaptability is a shared **currency** (adapt tokens, cap 6, starts 2, no refresh), not a Ledger track.
     Team Reflexivity flips when the pool holds 4+ at close of week. Adapt tokens left at the end
     count in the Team Score.
  2. The whole Ledger incl. **Standing and Capital opens in week 2**, and **Needs are read from
     week 2** (was week 3), so the Quality no-Work penalty bites a week earlier. Week 3 keeps
     the Zone lift and Flex/Overtime. Week 4 opens nothing.
  3. **Hero:** unchanged for everyone (once per game, needs 3 Motivation). Only **Competitive**
     (Base) has a reusable Hero, and every reuse still needs 3 Motivation. Rulebook now matches
     the printed Competitive card (tendency: another character's Hero costs you 1 Motivation).
  4. **Quality** (Need) loses 3 Motivation at close of any week with no Work card played (+1 if
     one was). The only Need that can cost you. A character the Learn/Celebrate exploit
     actually hurts.
  5. Quits are checked once per week, at close step 3. The weekly turn is now a checklist
     (rulebook §6.1, printed on every person board).
  6. **"Replace a card" removed** from the Adapt step: tokens only cancel Events. Autonomy's Need
     is now "you executed at least one card this week" (it used to depend on Replace).
  7. **Quality's Need counts Work delivered**, not a Work card played: +1 if Work was added to a
     feature this week, -3 if none was (a Work card that produced 0 does not count).
  8. **Vocabulary fixed** (rulebook §6.1): Shit Happens = the step and the board, Event = the
     card, Adapt = the next step, Cancel = what you do to an Event, "Adaptability cost" = the
     price printed on it. Board 2 no longer says an uncancelled Event "stays and the SHIT stacks".
  9. **Text size** raised on the person board, all habitus cards and the NEED and HERO tiles.
  10. **Legs and Head swapped flavour, not rules** (2026-10-07): Legs is now instinct (Pressure
      Response, Hero), Head is education (Zone row, Skill). The Hero tile moved under Legs on the
      person board and Flex under Head; Hero glyphs are now triangles, education rows hexagons.
  11. **Activity cards belong to the team** (2026-10-07): nobody plays or executes a card, so
      there are no character discs and **Skill match is removed**. Needs rewritten to match:
      Autonomy = no Event landed on the team (or Shared Repertoire), Hierarchy = the team played
      a Teach, Recognition = a feature was banked or you spent your Hero; Overtime is a sixth
      card the team plays. Every Need except Recognition also counts as met while the team has
      its vindicating Habit. The week-2 Capital catch-up is dropped: Capital starts at 0 and is earned
      only through play, since it assumed characters played cards.
  12. **Balance pass** (2026-10-07, expected values, not playtested): Event Adaptability cost now
      follows size (8 cards at 1, 11 at 2, 4 at 3), Habit takes 1 off with a minimum of 1,
      Sustainable threshold is 88, and the Work/Event figures in the rulebook were refreshed (12
      Events drawn, neutral rate 1 Work per card, ~21 Work days needed).
  13. **The Zone is tracked twice** (rulebook v0.32, 2026-10-08, never played). Every character
      has their own 5x5 in the SKILLS box on their person board (the SPECIMEN; the old Item
      space): whole box = the grid, label above it, Item diamonds below and Head hexagons to the
      right. The Zone board is the team's picture: 25 two-sided tiles, one-person face and
      stacked face (two or more cover the square). When someone Learns or is Taught they mark
      their SPECIMEN, then put down or flip a Zone tile. One start square (Head row x Item
      column); Learn left/right in your own row; Teach up/down/left/right of the *teacher's*
      square into any row; the team names the learner. The Zone pays: Capability +1 for a whole
      Zone row or column tiled, Shared Practice +1 when a tile flips to stacked, Cross-Trained
      Capital on a 3rd row of your own SPECIMEN. Removed: column Activity +1, the Teach+Learn
      combo, the empty-row rule, the sideways "new this week" card (now a two-faced counter).
      Stale "Skills modify Activities" section deleted. Person board also: Flex and Zone Start
      swapped; Motivation and Capital swapped (Motivation left); the numbers in the Motivation
      squares removed; Capital's joining line replaced by arrows between the circles; **the week
      is now seven steps** (Motivation - the grind, Torso, Need, quits - is its own step 7 after
      CLOSE). **To check at the next table:** did anyone forget the second step (the Zone tile)
      after marking their SPECIMEN; did a Teach or Learn take longer than a minute; does the
      Zone ever reach a full row or column; do stacks fire often enough to matter; is a 5mm
      counter workable in an 8.8 x 7.2mm cell; does a separate Motivation step stop the grind
      being forgotten.
  14. **Two Events about power** (rulebook v0.33, 2026-10-09, never played). **EVERYONE IS
      EQUAL** (Management, cost 2): no Hero, no Authorized Voice, no Capital gained this week,
      everyone -1 Motivation (Autonomy -2, Hierarchy 0). **THE WOLF** (Management): the character
      with the most Capital is targeted (they stay on the team; their Capital is at stake); it cannot be cancelled (no tokens, no Hero, no
      Authorized Voice). The team steps up - that character +1 Capital, every character -1
      Motivation - or their Capital is erased (goes to 0). Not a Hero play. If nobody has Capital, discard.
      Deck is 25 cards. **To check:** does the team step up every time (the cost is only 1 each),
      or does it let the leader fall; does the top-Capital character ever want to be the one who
      is not saved; is a 48% chance of meeting the Wolf in a game (12 of 25 cards drawn) too high.
  15. **Balance pass from the simulator** (rulebook v0.35, 2026-10-12, never played at a table).
      **Speak Up** is a once-a-game gift of +1 Motivation to any teammate, whenever, if Trust is over 2; it no
      longer reads Standing. **Own It** takes the blame for an uncancelled Event (lose 1 Motivation, team +1
      Trust, unlimited but one Trust step a week) and keeps only the low-Trust -1. **Take One For The Team is
      Dependable-only**, 2 Motivation for +1 Trust and +1 Capital. **Combos open from week 1.** **THE DATE
      MOVES UP is not in play in week 2.** Engage pays +1 in weeks 1-3, nothing in week 4, -1 in weeks 5-6;
      Authorized Voice flips at Standing 4; Workshop no longer meets the Community Need, and Psychological
      Safety meets it only when no Management or People Event landed. REORGANISED costs only Autonomy 1
      Motivation, EVERYTHING AT ONCE and EQUIPMENT OUTAGE only Hierarchy 1. Demo also gives its receiver +1
      Motivation and the team +1 Adaptability. Team Score thresholds are 5 x characters + 75 and + 125 (100
      and 150 at five players). The Zone back is the introduction, the Project back the final tally and the
      four results. **To check:** does a Speak Up holder spend the gift early or hold it for a quit; does Own
      It ever get played; is Trust too slow for a team with no Dependable character; is 100 reachable by a
      team that holds together and 150 only by a team that plays well.
- **Summary of the rule changes (v0.31, as of 2026-10-08).** Everything above in one place:
  - **Adaptability** is a shared pile of adapt tokens (cap 6, starts at 2, no refresh); Align,
    Workshop and Reflect (Trust 3+) each add 1 at close of week. "Replace a card" is gone.
  - **Staging:** week 1 is Work, Coordinate, Celebrate; the whole Ledger, Needs and Shit Happens
    open together in week 2 (so 12 Events are drawn, weeks 2-7); week 3 adds the Zone lift and
    Flex/Overtime.
  - **Activity cards belong to the team.** Nobody plays or executes a card: no character discs,
    no Skill match. Overtime is a sixth card the team plays (one character pays 1 Motivation and
    takes the Flex marker).
  - **Capital** starts at 0 for everyone and is earned only through play; no week-2 catch-up.
  - **Needs** (read from week 2, +1 Motivation when met): Autonomy = no Event landed on the team
    that week; Hierarchy = the team played a Teach; Community = the team spent a Coordinate,
    Reflect or Celebrate; Quality = Work was added to a feature (otherwise -3); Recognition = a
    feature was banked, or you spent your Hero. Every Need except Recognition also counts as met
    while the team has its vindicating Habit (Shared Repertoire, Who Knows What, Psychological
    Safety, Team Reflexivity).
  - **Events:** Adaptability cost follows size (cost 1: 8 cards, cost 2: 11, cost 3: 4; average
    1.8, was 2.8). A Habit takes 1 off, minimum 1. All eight "everyone loses 1 Motivation" cards
    print both the Torso that loses 1 extra and the Torso that is immune.
  - **Hero:** once per game for everyone (needs 3 Motivation); only the Competitive Base has a
    reusable Hero.
  - **Legs and Head** swapped flavour, not rules: Legs is instinct (Pressure Response, Hero),
    Head is education (Zone row).
  - **Scoring:** Sustainable High Performance now needs Team Score 88 (68 + 5 per starting
    character); 80 let a surviving team on a neutral Ledger reach it.
  - **What the numbers say (expected values, never played):** a neutral team needs about 21 Work
    days against about 22 available, so investing in Capability and Shared Practice is how the
    project finishes; roughly 4-5 Celebrates keep everyone above 0; buying out an Event with
    tokens is only worth it for the cost-1 and the biggest cards, while Trust 4 (a free cancel
    each week) and Hero are the better routes.
  - **Component-only changes:** lighter base colour on habitus, Need and Hero cards and the
    person-board seats; Need tiles headed NEED / HERO with the value name as a caption, and
    condition text tinted by Event category.
- **Open - not decided, do not treat as fixed:**
  - **Zone skill tracking** was the biggest unsolved component problem; redesigned in entry 13
    (skills on your own sheet). Untested - count this open until a table has played it.
  - Should the habit tracks reward from 2+ instead of penalising 1 and below?
  - Feature deadlines, and a cost to non-Work activity (to stop the all-Work/no-Work swing).
  - Consequence for quitting (lose condition, cascade, score hit); coordinate twice per week?
    An optional answer is drafted, untested, in `game/extensions/saboteur/` (a character at 0
    turns Saboteur instead of leaving).
  - Stacking Events: does an unhandled Event repeat next week until dealt with?
  - Whether the grind comes before or after Events: the rulebook has it at close of week
    (after Events); the table found the order unclear. Rule unchanged, order now printed.
  - Cards to retext: ALWAYS REACTING, the Celebrate card and the board 3 / board 2 Adaptability
    changes are done; the Quality Need tile is done. Still to do: the two BACK sheets and the
    Challenge Head cards (pool wording). Board 3's Standing slot and the 3b strip are done (the
    strip is single-faced now and drops in at week 2). The
    Competitive card and the Hero tiles match the rules.
- **Next thing to test:** week 1-3 with the exploit closed. Does anyone still play three
  Learn/Celebrate weeks, and does a shared Adaptability pool get spent or hoarded?

## Session 1 plan (first playtest) - play **Lite**, not v0.1
The [playability assessment](../docs/playability-assessment.md) found v0.1 unplayable as
written (no numbers on most Activities, ambiguous action currency, a work budget using under
20% of capacity, and a deadlocked Capital loop). Session 1 therefore runs
[../rules/rulebook-lite.md](../rules/rulebook-lite.md): 15 days, 5 Activities, 2 tracks, no
Capital, all-paper components, 45-60 minutes.

Full session instructions are in Lite §11. In short:

- **Players:** 4, mixed experience - at least one person outside the hobby. (Testing only
  with hobbyists hides onboarding problems.)
- **Who teaches:** a named rules-explainer actively coaches Week 1 (days 1-5), then
  deliberately steps back for days 6-15. Note the moment help stopped being needed.
- **Build the habitus columns before explaining anything** (Lite §3) - five paper part-cards
  per player. Nothing is 3D-printed; the build ritual itself is under test.
- **What to build first:** habitus part cards, 4 feature cards, the Trust/Capability tracks,
  the day track, and the 12 Event cards. Roughly 20 minutes of index-card
  work.
- **Length:** play all 15 days. Lite is sized to finish.

**Answer these three, explicitly, in the log:** Q1 is a day under 3 minutes? Q2 was Work ever
*not* the obvious action? Q3 did the habitus build change anyone's behaviour? If Q2 comes back
"Work was always right," stop and fix the action economy before tuning anything else.

## What to listen for (don't just record outcomes - record what people say)
These are the moments that matter more than whether players can name the theory behind them:
- *"We actually had enough time. We just spent it badly."*
- *"We should have taught [X] that in week two."*
- *"We became really efficient, but when the requirements changed we were in trouble."*
- *"Nobody challenged him because he had all the status."*
- *"We delivered everything, but that team is completely burned out."*

## Debrief questions
- **Fun:** Did we enjoy making decisions? Did we feel pressure? Any memorable moments?
- **Strategy:** Were there real trade-offs? Was Work always the obvious choice? Were the
  non-Work activities worth the time?
- **Team dynamics:** Did Trust actually matter? Did sharing knowledge matter? Did Habitus
  visibly change how people behaved?
- **Theory:** Did the game feel anything like Wenger's community of practice? Did social
  learning actually matter? Did Habitus create real behaviour patterns?
- **Balance:** Is the project too easy or impossible? Are Events too harsh? Is onboarding
  worth the time? Is rest worth the time?
