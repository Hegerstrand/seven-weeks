HIGH-PERFORMANCE TEAM · HOW TO PLAY

PAGE 1 OF 2 · SET UP, AND THE WEEK

Seven weeks. Five days a week. Six features (A–F) to deliver before week 7 closes. You commit all five days before you know what is coming, resolve them together, then close the week. From week 2, Events crash into the plan you already made. Everything is cooperative: the five Activity cards are the team's - nobody plays one. Deliver everything with a Team Score of 88+ and you did not only finish the project, you built a team that could do it again.

SET UP · ABOUT FIFTEEN MINUTES

  1. Deal and build. Each player is dealt five habitus parts at random - Base, Legs, Torso, Head, Item - and slots them into the seats on their person board (the SPECIMEN). Nobody chooses.
  2. Take your tiles. The NEED tile that names your Torso and the HERO tile that names your Legs.
  3. Start square. Mark one square on your SKILLS grid: where your Head's row (education) meets your Item's column (skill). Then put a tile on the same square of THE ZONE (stacked face up if two of you start on the same square).
  4. Pick a Project card, turn THE PROJECT face up and seat it. It names features A–F. Read their cost, complexity and locks: D and F cannot be placed until what they sit on is built.
  5. Motivation 3 and Capital 0 for everyone. Week marker on 1. Shuffle the Events, do not draw yet.
  6. Only THE PROJECT and THE ZONE start face up. SHIT HAPPENS and THE TEAM stay text side up; they turn over in week 2.

YOUR PERSON BOARD

  • Habitus seats - who you are. Nothing is written down: the figure is the record.
  • Zone Start / SKILLS grid - your own 5×5: education rows × skill columns. A counter in a square means you can do that. Item diamonds sit below the grid, Head hexagons beside it.
  • Hero (under Legs) - once a game, cancel an Event that names your Pressure Response. +2 Capital to you, +1 Motivation to another. Needs 3 Motivation.
  • Need (under Torso) - from week 2, +1 Motivation if your Torso's condition was met this week.
  • Flex - the marker for a day you worked as Overtime and owe back.
  • Motivation (5 squares, starts 3) and Capital (0–5, a marker you slide, only grows when the team witnesses it). THE WEEK checklist is printed down the side.

THE WEEK · SEVEN STEPS, EVERY WEEK

1PLAN - lay five Activity cards, Monday to Friday. Nothing resolves. A slot may take the Flex marker instead (Overtime, see page 2).
2SHIT HAPPENS (week 2 on) - draw 2 Events, read them out. Nothing resolves yet.
3ADAPT - cancel Events by paying each one's Adaptability cost (1, 2 or 3) in adapt tokens, or play a Hero. A Habit takes 1 off the cost (minimum 0: a cost-1 Event is free).
4SURVIVING EVENTS HAPPEN - resolve every Event nobody cancelled.
5THE WEEK RUNS - resolve the five cards in any order. The week happened at once.
6CLOSE - bank finished features (lift any lock, pay Capital to who worked on it) → walk the Ledger, at most one step per track → add this week's adapt tokens to the pool (Align, Workshop, Reflect; cap 6).
7MOTIVATION - the grind. One net number per character: −1 for the grind, ±1 per Event category your Torso is hit by or immune to, +1 if your Need was met. Apply it, then anyone on 0 quits. This is the only moment a quit is checked. Move the week marker.
WEEK 1 IS ONLY STEPS 1, 5, 6 AND 7. Lay five cards from Work, Coordinate, Celebrate, resolve them, bank anything finished, take the grind. No Events, no Ledger, no Needs yet. A week is five days and you choose them - that is the entire idea.

WHAT OPENS, AND WHEN

WEEKWHAT TURNS UP
1The week. Work, Coordinate, Celebrate.
2THE TEAM turns over: Trust, Capability, Shared Practice, Standing, Capital, Needs, the adapt pool (starts at 3 tokens), Shit Happens and Adapt.
3THE DEVELOPMENT (Learn/Teach payoff), Flex and Overtime. Now also Learn, Teach, Align, Reflect, Workshop, Plan, Demo.
4Nothing new. Play everything you have met.
5Combos: two named triggers in the same week pay a small bonus (one combo a week).
6–7Nothing new. The project ends when week 7 closes.

THE ACTIVITIES · ONE CARD = ONE DAY

The cards belong to the team. Nothing here is "may": if a number is not listed, nothing happens.

CARDEXACT EFFECT
Work+1 to one feature's Work counter (Capability changes it; never below 0). Not on a feature whose dependency is not yet banked.
PlanPick a feature: every Work card on it this week gives +1 extra. High complexity: also next week.
Coordinate+1 Trust.
AlignThe meeting nobody wants. Every character pays 1 Motivation; the team gets +2 adapt tokens at the close, spendable from next week. Nothing if anyone is on 1. One way only.
Workshop+1 adapt token, no conditions. Arrives at the close, spendable from next week.
ReflectNeeds Trust 3+. +1 adapt token. Look back at last week: a feature was banked (◎), you adapted to 2 Events (↝), or a Hero played / an Event cost Trust, Capability or Shared Practice (↻)? Then also +1 Shared Practice, and the token may go in the matching Team Reflexivity well.
LearnEvery character marks a free square left or right of one they cover, in their own row (up to five new squares). Then tile the Zone for each. No Capital.
TeachUp to two teachers; each lets one other character mark a square up, down, left or right of one the teacher covers, any row. Then tile the Zone. Both teachers +1 Capital. Dead while Trust ≤ 1.
DemoNeeds Capital ≥ the team average. The team names one character: +1 Capital. The feature ignores the next Negative Feedback.
Celebrate+1 Motivation to everyone (cap 5) - +2 if a feature was banked this week.

Overtime. A sixth card the team may lay: one character pays 1 Motivation and takes the Flex marker, and the team owes that day back later. Unrepaid Flex costs 2 at scoring.

NEEDS · FROM WEEK 2

At step 7 you get +1 Motivation if your Torso's Need was met this week. Each also counts as met while the team holds its Habit.

AutonomyAt most 1 Event landed on the team.
HierarchyThe team played a Teach.
CommunityThe team spent a Coordinate, Reflect or Celebrate.
QualityWork was added to a feature. Otherwise −3.
RecognitionA feature was banked, or you spent your Hero.

THE ZONE · TWO THINGS EVERY TIME

Whenever someone Learns or is Taught, do two things, in this order:

1Mark it on the SPECIMEN - a counter on that character's own SKILLS grid. The coloured face means "new this week": it cannot be taught until the week closes.
2Cover it on THE ZONE - no tile there? Put one down. A tile already there? Flip it.
one dot - one of you covers this square two dots - stacked: two or more cover it

Counters are never taken off and tiles never flip back. The Ledger reads the Zone only at the moment a tile goes down or flips. Your sheet is the record of what I can do; the Zone is the team's picture of the same squares. Everyone can read everyone's sheet.

THE LEDGER READS THE ZONE
Capability +1A whole row or column of the Zone now has a tile on every square (either face).
Shared Practice +1A tile flips to its stacked face - two of you now cover the same square.
Cross-TrainedThe first time your own SKILLS grid has squares in a 3rd row: +1 Capital, once each.

THE LEDGER AND MOTIVATION

  • Trust, Capability, Shared Practice - coins on THE TEAM (scale 0–5), one step a week each. A coin that reaches its ring turns into a Habit: it makes one Event category cheaper to cancel, and satisfies a Need.
  • Adaptability is a shared pile of tokens (cap 6). Spend them in step 3.
  • Capital is earned, never given: banked work, a Demo, a Teach, a Hero, Cross-Trained. Rich in Capital = standing in the team.
  • Motivation is personal. The grind takes 1 every week; Celebrate and a met Need give it back. At 0 you quit and the team scores −20.

HOW IT ENDS · SCORING

TEAM SCORE = features banked × 10 + (Trust + Capability + Shared Practice + adapt tokens, max 21) + all Motivation − 20 × quits − 2 × unrepaid Flex − 5 × tracks left at 0 or 1

Checked when week 7 closes. 88 = 68 + 5 for every character you started with.

FailureNot all six features banked. The score does not matter.
Heroic SuccessAll six, score under 88. The project made it. The team did not.
High PerformanceAll six, score 88+. You built a team as well.
